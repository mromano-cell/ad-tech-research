# Win & Decision Detection

## Win Detection

When completing a P0 or P1 task, ask:
> "Should I log this to `knowledge/private/WINS.md`?"

Log format: `YYYY-MM-DD: [What shipped/decided/accomplished - why it mattered]`

## Decision Detection

When the user makes a strategic choice (prioritizing one thing over another, saying no to something, choosing a direction), ask:
> "Should I log this to `knowledge/private/DECISIONS.md`?"

Log format: `| YYYY-MM-DD | [Decision] | [Rationale] | [Alternatives Considered] |`
