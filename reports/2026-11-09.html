Weekly Narrative

This Week's TL;DR

The week's biggest story is that AI-driven search and conversational interfaces are not yet monetizing as well as traditional search. Google said lower monetization in AI Mode weighed on Search growth, and a new study found ChatGPT ads generate 64% less revenue per visit than Google Search. Meanwhile, Amazon is placing Sponsored Product ads inside text-based shopping agent replies. For marketers, ads are moving into conversations faster than the economics and measurement are maturing, which makes proven high-intent inventory more valuable in the near term.

AI & Search

  • Google said lower monetization in AI Mode weighed on Search growth. This is a rare admission that AI answers are cannibalizing ad yield. Search was the top ad format this week (6 articles), and Google was the most-mentioned company (5 articles).
  • A new study found ChatGPT ads generate 64% less revenue per visit than Google Search. The finding tempers expectations for conversational advertising, which was the #1 trending topic (4 articles).
  • OpenAI is moving toward brand-facing analytics: ChatGPT is about to show brands their mention count. This would be an early step toward "share of voice" reporting for AI answers.
  • Publishers face a harder trade-off as Google's AI visibility push complicates the opt-out decision while they wait for click data. Digiday also explains the new AI training opt-out policy token.
  • Reddit reported Q3 results amid a major traffic shift. Its search-referred traffic is increasingly shaped by AI-driven discovery.

Programmatic & Data

  • Q3 earnings showed ad tech's widening divide: AppLovin surged while rivals struggled, so scale and proprietary performance data continue to separate winners from the pack.
  • Apple's Safari crackdown is pushing ad tech toward server-side solutions, though doubts remain about how durable those workarounds will be. Cookieless and ID solutions each surfaced in coverage this week.
  • Meta's $25 billion privacy fine in New Mexico is a headline-grabbing figure. AdExchanger questions whether it will meaningfully change ad tech practices.
  • Digital is approaching 75% share of local ad spend, heading toward 80% by 2030. This reinforces the long-term shift of local budgets into addressable channels.

Retail Media

  • Amazon's ad revenue growth decelerated due to Prime Day tweaks. The slowdown shows how sensitive retail media growth is to tentpole event timing. Retail Media Ads appeared in 4 articles this week.
  • Amazon's shopping agent now takes orders by text, with Sponsored Product ads in the replies. This is one of the clearest live examples of ads embedded in conversational commerce.
  • Digiday breaks down how the grocery store became a media channel, as in-store and grocer networks compete for CPG trade and media dollars.
  • Marketplace platform Mirakl rebranded for the AI age, positioning itself for agent-driven commerce and retail media enablement.

Attribution & Measurement

  • Google Ads added an 'All Conversions' tab to simplify conversion tracking and audits. The change gives advertisers a consolidated view across conversion actions.
  • Performance metrics are rewriting creator rate cards, pushing the creator economy (3 articles) and influencer marketing (3 articles) toward outcome-based pricing.
  • Creators see promise in Instagram's long-form push but question whether exposure alone translates into measurable value.
  • Despite creators' big year, Christmas campaigns still went to celebrities. Examples include Quince (David Schwimmer) and Xfinity (John C. Reilly).

Agencies

  • Forrester's Wave report finds growing homogeneity among the 12 biggest agencies, making differentiation harder for holding companies. WPP led agency mentions with 3 articles.
  • WPP's Ogilvy and CAA launched a partnership to clone celebrity talent for AI ads, combining celebrity likeness rights with generative production.
  • Pernod Ricard USA hired WPP's VML as commerce agency of record, another signal that commerce media is becoming a standalone agency discipline.
  • Digiday's Media Buying Summit recap weighs the upsides and challenges of AI spreading across media agencies, including Publicis.

Yelp Impact Analysis

This week brought no direct moves from Google Local Services Ads, Nextdoor, or Apple Business Connect. The closest adjacent trend is still highly relevant: AI search surfaces are monetizing worse than classic Google Search, and ChatGPT ads yield 64% less revenue per visit. That gap strengthens the case for proven high-intent platforms where consumers actively search for restaurants and service providers and are ready to convert. The market is also growing, with digital nearing 75% of local ad spend on its way to 80% by 2030. Amazon's sponsored ads inside agent replies offer a template for how conversational local discovery ads might work, and ChatGPT's brand mention counts could soon put pressure on local platforms to report AI-era visibility. For managed-service and programmatic products aimed at multi-location businesses, Apple's Safari crackdown and Google's 'All Conversions' tab raise the bar for durable, server-side, location-level conversion reporting, which Yelp should prioritize to defend its share of local budgets.
