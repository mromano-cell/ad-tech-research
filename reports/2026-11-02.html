Weekly Narrative

This Week's TL;DR

AI is now built into the core of search and commerce advertising. Google led the week's coverage with 9 mentions as it added AI-generated review summaries to Search ads, expanded AI Overviews on commercial queries, and floated using Gmail and Photos data for targeting. Meanwhile, ChatGPT's advertiser base jumped 67% in two weeks, and Amazon pointed to agentic AI as its next ads growth engine. High-intent discovery is shifting from links and listings toward AI-mediated answers and agents, so platforms that own trusted intent signals and reviews need to show measurable value inside those new surfaces.

AI & Search

  • Google confirmed it is adding AI-generated review summaries to Search ads. Advertisers' review content now appears directly inside paid units, which raises the stakes for anyone whose value rests on reviews.
  • A new study found AI Overviews nearly doubled for commercial keywords, pushing traditional ads and organic results further down the page. Search was the top ad format this week, appearing in 8 articles.
  • ChatGPT is scaling as an ad channel quickly. Advertisers on the platform jumped 67% in just 14 days, a sign that budgets are testing conversational search as an alternative to Google.
  • Buyer sentiment is changing. Google search is losing its "free pass" with buyers, who are scrutinizing its performance more closely as AI reshapes the results page.
  • Google Ads expanded Smart Bidding Exploration to Performance Max and Shopping campaigns, giving its automated bidding more room to chase incremental queries.

Programmatic & Data

  • Google said it might use Gmail and Google Photos data to target ads. The move would significantly expand its first-party signal pool and is likely to draw privacy scrutiny.
  • Publicis closed its $2.2 billion acquisition of LiveRamp and dismissed concerns that competing agencies will leave the platform. Data clean rooms appeared in 2 articles this week.
  • Regulation is tightening. Pennsylvania will require labels on AI-generated ads, and Google rewrote its ad rules protecting children online.
  • People Inc. is building custom APIs so AI agents can more easily use its journalism. Publishers are positioning their content as structured inventory for the agent economy.
  • Meta reported Q3 results showing it boosted AI spend alongside growth in its ad business and users, reinforcing its push toward AI-driven ad automation.

CTV & Video

  • AI ad-placement companies Rembrand and Whisper World merged and are eyeing an IPO, a bet on AI-inserted brand placements within video content. CTV/OTT and Video each appeared in 3 articles.
  • Creator economy was the top trending topic, with 4 mentions. Creator agency LV8 merged with sister firm Mirage and raised "high eight figures".
  • The Giggly Squad's SiriusXM deal signals a shift from podcast networks toward multi-format creator ecosystems spanning audio and video.
  • Holiday creative season is underway. Adweek rounded up the 2026 holiday ads worth watching, including campaigns from e.l.f. Cosmetics, Levi's, and Kay.

Retail Media

  • Amazon Ads neared $20 billion in Q3 revenue and is betting on agentic AI to fuel further growth. Agentic commerce appeared in 2 articles this week.
  • Amazon said more than 400M customers have used Alexa for Shopping in the last 12 months, which positions voice and AI assistants as real shopping surfaces.
  • Google is pitching itself as a commerce "matchmaker" rather than a retailer, framing its ad business as a neutral connector between shoppers and sellers in the AI shopping era.

Attribution & Measurement

  • Apple's latest Safari block hands WPP a fresh case for InfoSum, its clean-room technology. ID Solutions led attribution topics with 3 mentions.
  • SMBs recognize AI search visibility as critical but struggle to measure its impact, leaving a measurement gap that ad platforms can fill.
  • A startup is working to ensure AI tools aren't out of reach for SMBs. SMB advertising appeared in 2 articles this week.
  • The Publicis–LiveRamp deal (see coverage) consolidates a key identity and measurement layer under a holding company. That could change how neutral clean-room collaboration feels to rival agencies.

Agencies

  • Stagwell's Mark Penn laid out a plan to avoid holding company bloat. Stagwell was the most-mentioned agency, with 2 articles.
  • Amid growing tension over workplace AI, agencies have a window to reimagine staffing.
  • Attendees at the Media Buying Summit discussed how AI is both helping and hurting client relations and office work.
  • WPP is using Apple's Safari changes to strengthen the pitch for InfoSum, while Publicis now owns LiveRamp. Data infrastructure is becoming a core competitive weapon for holding companies.

Yelp Impact Analysis

The most direct competitive signal this week is Google bringing AI-generated review summaries into Search ads while AI Overviews nearly double on commercial keywords. Together, these let Google synthesize review-driven trust signals for restaurants and service businesses without sending users to third-party review platforms. The 67% surge in ChatGPT advertisers and Amazon's agentic shopping push add another high-intent discovery channel competing for local ad budgets. There was no specific news this week on Google Local Services Ads, Nextdoor, or Apple Business Connect. However, the finding that SMBs struggle to measure AI search visibility is an opening for Yelp to package closed-loop, lead-level attribution as a differentiator for local and multi-location advertisers. That proof is also increasingly needed as Safari tracking restrictions erode third-party measurement. Finally, Pennsylvania's AI-generated ad labeling requirement and Google's expanded Smart Bidding Exploration in Performance Max suggest that managed-service and programmatic products for multi-location brands will need AI-creative compliance controls and transparent performance reporting to compete with Google's increasingly automated campaigns.
