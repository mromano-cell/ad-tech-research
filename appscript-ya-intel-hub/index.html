What Makes YA Segments Different from DSP Audiences
1
Declared Intent via Search
Yelp search is someone explicitly saying "I want X." No inference, no modeling from passive browsing.
DSPs infer interest from browsing and purchase history. Yelp captures it directly.
2
Real-Time Recency
Yelp knows someone searched today or this hour. Catches people in the moment of intent, not weeks later.
DSP segments use 30-90 day lookback windows. Yelp's signal is hours old, not months.
3
1,500+ Category Depth
"Searched for acai bowls" is more specific and actionable than being tagged "health enthusiast."
DSPs group users into broad interest buckets. Yelp targets the exact category they searched.
4
Competitive Conquesting
Someone searching a competitor by name on Yelp (e.g., Club Pilates targeting users who searched "Orange Theory").
DSPs cannot see competitor-specific named searches. Yelp can.
5
Price-Point Signaling
$ vs. $$$$ filter engagement reveals spending intent. Target by how much someone is willing to spend.
No DSP equivalent. Price-tier behavior is unique to Yelp's platform.
6
Cross-Category Behavioral Combos
Combine unrelated searches to infer lifestyle: "dog groomers" + "hiking trails" = active outdoor pet owner.
DSPs see one dimension at a time. Yelp combines multiple declared intents into rich profiles.
7
Timing + Intent + Location Layered Together
Dayparting, proximity to a business, and active search stacked. Most powerful when all three fire at once. Someone searching "sushi" at 6pm within 2 miles of a restaurant is as close to a guaranteed visit as advertising gets.
DSPs can do location OR timing OR interest. Yelp layers all three from a single declared action.

Every day, 76 million people come to Yelp with a decision to make. Not a product to scroll past. Not a banking app to check. A real, local decision: which dentist to call, which contractor to hire, which restaurant to book tonight.

When they search on Yelp, they tell us exactly what they want. When they read reviews, they tell us they are serious. When they compare providers, they tell us they are choosing. That progression (search, research, decide) is the highest-value signal in local commerce. And it only exists on Yelp.

Yelp Audiences puts your brand in front of that moment. Not after the transaction. Not after the visit. Before, when the decision is still being made and your brand can be the answer.
