## Known Issues & Tensions

1. **Sales contacting TTD directly.** Per Sam: "We have always operated that sales should not be contacting TTD on their own without YCM. IDK when that changed but that is why we're dealing with all this blowback."

2. **Newsletter action ownership unclear.** TTD sends monthly product pitches and upsell recommendations to a broad Yelp distro. Nobody clearly owns evaluating and acting on these.

3. **YCM vs. CD role confusion on TTD.** Sam: "This is why YCMs should own YA. I've been screaming into a void asking my manager what CD is doing with this. YCMs are not responsible for upsell or selling."

4. **Seller TTD access.** Sam is "highly against sellers getting TTD access" due to risk of sharing screenshots, pushing for lower margins. Sarah Chang's post-EBR action item requested view-only access for sales reps.

5. **Audience enhancement outreach bypassed PMM.** Jenny Kao (TTD) reached out directly to Mel in Jan 2026 (via Anil). PMM wasn't looped in until Mike asked Mel about it in Aug.

6. **Reactive vs. proactive.** EBR Gemini notes flagged the team needs to "transition from reactive tool implementation" to a proactive strategy.
