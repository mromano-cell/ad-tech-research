The `knowledge/` folder should contain all the personal, world and Yelp knowledge you'd like your LLM to have access to. 

Consider adding files describing Yelp jargon, your team's charter or strategy, descriptions of frequently used user segments or personas, past projects, status quo screenshots of key flows, etc.

## Subfolders

- `private/` — Your goals, wins, decisions, and stakeholders.
- `voice/` — Your writing style guides. These inform how your LLM writes on your behalf.
- `templates/` — PEP and framework doc templates.
