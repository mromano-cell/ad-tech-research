# Qualities of a Good Framework Review

A reference document for what strong strategic framework reviews look like. Use this as a checklist when preparing or reviewing a framework.

---

## Operational Context

- Usually happens **1 to 2 months before** design/engineering starts on the initiative.
- Allows time to align with cross-org stakeholders and leadership before execution.
- Aim for a max of 2 iterations (including the first one):
  - First conversation: "How is everyone thinking about this?" — an assimilation of perspectives.
  - Second conversation: A thought-through result of the first conversation with the qualities outlined below.

---

## Qualities of a Strong Framework

**1. User Problem: Psychology & Choreography**

What user psychology, coverage of content, and user flow need to be strung together to capture the opportunity?

*Note: "Psychology & Choreography" is more precise than "user flow." It captures:*
- **Psychology**: Cognitive friction, confusion, attitude, motivation before and during the experience
- **Choreography**: Physical actions (thumb-scrolling, tapping, eye-scanning, back-button hiking)

A good investment proposal maps these out crisply.

**2. Ecosystem Mapping**

Map execution levers to the user psychology/choreography above. Nice to have: rough low-fidelity designs that showcase this mapping.

**3. Risks & Challenges**

Takes a stand on:
- The hardest parts to build
- The riskiest assumptions in the choreography

**4. What Do You Have to Believe (WDYHTB)**

Maps choreography to **dent**:
- This is a WDYHTB model, not a predictive model.
- In its "fully baked" state, how much of a dent will it make — on users, on the company, on the market?
- Clearly play out what needs to happen for the dent to happen.

**5. Market Research**

What does the market say? Share interpretations of what you observe. Is there anything in the market that can inspire the approach?

**6. Level of Effort / Sequencing**

Takes a stand on fast vs. slow parts:
- Which part of the choreography should be hacked vs. built right?

**7. Prioritization / Now, Next, Later**

Based on the ecosystem mapping and risks, which part of the choreography should be built first?

---

## Candidates for Async Framework Review

1. Need a review before a board or leadership meeting — good to do async rather than wait for a slot.
2. Need for speed — if the regular review slot is too far out.
3. A smaller, faster gut-check on a second iteration rather than a full review.

---

## Tactical Best Practices

1. Show visual mocks with the concept.
2. Have a designated note taker.
3. Bring in data people early.
4. Plan on coming back twice.
5. Make slides a question, not a statement.
