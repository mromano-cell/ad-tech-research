## Software Engineer Persona Ideas

Beyond dining and fitness, here are targeting angles for reaching SWEs:

**Lifestyle & Interests:**
- Coffee shops / specialty coffee (remote work, meetings)
- Co-working spaces / workspace searches
- Happy hour / cocktail bars (team outings, after-work)
- Bike shops / cycling (commuter culture in tech hubs)
- Pet services (dog-friendly offices, WFH pet owners)

**Life Stage:**
- Home services (first-time homebuyers in late 20s-30s, high earners)
- Childcare / family activities (slightly older SWEs)
- Auto services / EV charging (Tesla/Rivian skew in tech)

**High-Intent Signals:**
- Late-night food delivery searches (crunch time / on-call)
- Weekend brunch (tech social culture)
- Travel & experiences (high disposable income, fewer kids on average)
- Health & wellness (meditation studios, massage, recovery)

**B2B Proxy Signals:**
- Conference/event venue searches (team offsites)
- Catering searches (office events)
- Corporate gift / flower delivery

These could be packaged as "The Software Engineer" persona bundle — multiple segments layered to reach SWEs through their off-work behavior on Yelp.
